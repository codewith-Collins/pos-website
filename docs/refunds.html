Refund policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Software licence

If the POS does not work for your shop, ask for a refund within 14 days of paying and we will refund the licence fee in full, by the same method you paid, within 14 days. No reason is needed. After that, refunds are given if we cannot fix a fault we have confirmed within 30 days of you reporting it.

Installation and support

  • Installation that has already been done is not refunded, unless the POS could not be made to work.
  • Prepaid support for a period that has not started is refunded in full; for a started year, the full months left are refunded.

How to ask

Contact Collin Muigai: murucollins@gmail.com or [support phone]. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2012 are not affected.

Returns in shops using the POS

This policy covers buying the POS software. Each shop sets its own returns policy for goods it sells; ask the shop.
