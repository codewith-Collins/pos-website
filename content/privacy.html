The POS software: who is responsible

{{name}} ("we") makes the {{product}} point-of-sale software ("the POS"). It is installed on a computer in each shop and runs there. We do not receive, store or have access to the shop's data: the POS sends no usage statistics, analytics or data to us or anyone else, and we have no remote access.

The shop decides what it records and why, so the shop is the data controller for its customers' and staff's personal data under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. If a shop asks us for support that needs access to its data, we act only on its written instructions, as a data processor.

How long it is kept

  • Sales, payment, return and stock records: at least 5 years, as Kenyan tax law requires.
  • Sign-in and security events: 12 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Password reset requests: 90 days after they are closed.
  • Customer details: until the customer asks for them to be erased.
  • Backups: the shop's most recent nightly backups (30 by default).

Your rights

You may ask a shop for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to erase it, and you may withdraw consent. The shop's administrator can download your data or erase your name, phone number and KRA PIN in the POS. Past sales stay in the shop's records without your details, because tax law requires them. You may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (www.odpc.go.ke).

Security

Data stays on the shop's computer in a database only the POS can open. Passwords are hashed, sign-ins lock after repeated wrong passwords, every important action is recorded, and backups are checked nightly. Traffic between devices on the shop's own network is not encrypted (no HTTPS) in this version, so shops should protect their Wi-Fi with a strong password and give customers a separate guest network.

Sharing and transfers

The POS shares data with no one. If a shop chooses a cloud-synced folder (for example Google Drive or OneDrive) for a backup copy, that provider stores the backups, possibly outside Kenya; that is the shop's choice under the Act.
