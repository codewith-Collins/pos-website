This website

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It has no analytics, advertising or third-party scripts, so there is nothing to accept or reject.

The POS software

The POS uses one cookie and two browser settings, all needed for it to work or to remember your choice. There are no tracking, analytics or advertising cookies.

NameTypePurposeKept for
pos_refreshCookie (essential)Keeps you signed in. A random code sent only to the POS sign-in service, not readable by page scripts.12 hours, or until you log out
pos-themeBrowser storageRemembers light or dark mode on this device, if you choose one.Until changed or cleared
pos-cookie-noticeBrowser storageRemembers that you have seen the cookie notice.Until cleared

You can delete them in your browser's settings at any time; deleting the cookie signs you out.
